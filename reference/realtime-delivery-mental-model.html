Realtime delivery: the four-part mental model

NotebookThe durable event log contains what actually happened.
To-do slipThe outbox remembers that committed news still needs announcing.
DoorbellThe WebSocket wakes a browser quickly, but is not the record.
BookmarkEach member cursor records the last sequence their view applied.

The invariant

Write the notebook and to-do slip first. Ring the doorbell afterward. On every ring or reconnect, read everything after the bookmark. Never infer history from whether a doorbell was heard.

Failure meanings

  • Browser offline: reconnect and read after its bookmark.
  • Wake-up lost: retry the durable outbox.
  • Web restarted: execution and durable history continue elsewhere.
  • Worker outcome unknown: pause for review; do not replay side effects.
