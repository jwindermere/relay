Walk through the realtime AgentRun prototype

Your goal: decide whether both pilot members remain correctly informed when realtime delivery fails. You are reviewing behavior, not terminal styling.

Database = notebookAuthoritative, numbered history.
WebSocket = doorbellFast notification that may disappear.
Cursor = bookmarkWhere each member last read.

Start

npm run prototype:realtime-agent-run

Enter one letter, press Return, and inspect the new screen. Use z to reset and q to quit.

1. Normal operation

Press a three times. Expect durable sequence 3, status working, and both cursors at 3.

Meaning: both members saw the same committed history.

2. Offline member and lost wake-up

Press 2, then a, then m. Pilot B stays behind; the outbox becomes pending. Press o: Pilot A catches up. Press 2: Pilot B reconnects and catches up. Press d: no duplicate update should appear.

Meaning: missed and repeated notifications do not lose or duplicate durable history.

3. Web restart

Press x. Both members go offline, but the durable AgentRun is unchanged. Press 1 and 2; both return to the latest sequence.

Meaning: the web process is only a delivery path, not the owner of execution.

4. Unknown worker outcome

Press z, press a three times, then press k. Expect recovering followed by paused.

Meaning: Relay does not automatically replay repository work when it cannot prove what the earlier attempt did.

Your verdict

Ask: Is this understandable enough for both pilot members? Is any update too noisy or missing? Should an unknown worker outcome pause, or should some other behavior occur?
